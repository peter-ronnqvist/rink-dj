Privacy Policy for RinkDJ

Last updated: 21 September 2026

RinkDJ (“the app”) is a game-day music controller for ice hockey. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: RinkDJ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third-party servers. There is no RinkDJ account and no backend.

Data stored on your device

All of your settings and content stay on your device, inside the app’s own storage:

  • Your team name, accent colour, and any logo image you import
  • Music files (MP3/WAV) you import and the playlists you build from them
  • Which track or Spotify link is bound to each game event

This data is never sent to us. It is removed if you delete the app.

Spotify (optional)

Connecting a Spotify account is entirely optional; the app is fully usable with local audio files and requires no login.

If you choose to connect Spotify:

  • Authorisation is handled by the Spotify app on your device. RinkDJ receives an access token, which is stored only on your device and used to control playback and to list the names of your playlists so you can pick them.
  • RinkDJ communicates directly with Spotify (api.spotify.com) for these features. Your Spotify credentials and access token are never sent to the developer — RinkDJ has no server.
  • Your use of Spotify is also governed by Spotify’s Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect Spotify at any time from within the app.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

RinkDJ contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. We do not profile you and do not use any third-party tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

RinkDJ does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
